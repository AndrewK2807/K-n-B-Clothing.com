Our Heritage & Craft

Elevated Streetwear Forged in Southern Africa.

K 'n' B Clothing began with a simple mission: to craft heavyweight, architectural streetwear that honors Basotho resilience and contemporary Southern African youth culture without compromising on luxury construction.

Every boxy tee is cut from combed 280 GSM cotton jersey, our denim is enzyme-washed for a vintage drape, and our fleece hoodies are brushed for warmth during sub-zero Maloti winters.
